Grow R from small to large. On a smooth surface the intersection stays a small closed loop; near a ridge, a saddle, or the edge of the patch, it breaks into separate arcs.

(x − a)² + (y − b)² + (f(x, y) − f(a, b))² = R²

The probe sphere is centred on P, not on the origin, and the surface is redrawn relative to P so the intersection is easy to read. For the sphere and torus presets the parameters are angles: u and v in radians.
